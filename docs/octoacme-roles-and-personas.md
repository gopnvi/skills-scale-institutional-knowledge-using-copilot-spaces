# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsor

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors provide strategic direction and sponsorship for the initiative. They align projects with business goals, authorize investments, and resolve cross-functional trade-offs that exceed team-level authority.

### Responsibilities
- Define strategic goals and success outcomes
- Approve scope, budget, and resourcing trade-offs
- Escalate unresolved risks or dependency issues
- Sponsor stakeholder alignment and decision-making
- Review milestones and benefits realization

### Goals
- Connect delivery work to business value
- Support timely decisions and prioritization
- Ensure program outcomes are realized

### Typical Communication
- Steering committee updates
- Executive briefings and business reviews
- Scope and priority approval discussions

### Interacts with existing roles
- Works with Project Managers to confirm priorities, milestones, and escalations.
- Aligns with Product Managers to clarify initiative outcomes and trade-offs.
- Supports Team Leads and Developers by clearing strategic blockers and approving major decisions.

---

## Delivery Manager

### Role Summary
Delivery Managers coordinate execution across teams, dependencies, and delivery rhythms. They keep work moving, identify bottlenecks, and help teams deliver predictable outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Manage cross-team dependencies and execution risks
- Maintain delivery health and milestone tracking
- Coordinate delivery planning and resource balancing
- Surface blockers and schedule impacts early
- Help teams resolve sequencing and prioritization issues

### Goals
- Improve delivery predictability
- Reduce dependency and bottleneck friction
- Align execution with agreed milestones

### Typical Communication
- Cross-team planning and dependency reviews
- Weekly delivery dashboards
- Risk and blocker escalation updates

### Interacts with existing roles
- Partners with Project Managers on plans, risks, and communication.
- Works with Product Managers to confirm sequencing and priority shifts.
- Coordinates closely with Developers and Team Leads to track progress and resolve execution issues.

---

## Functional Lead

### Role Summary
Functional Leads represent domain expertise, business capabilities, or technical specialties that are critical to project success. They validate that solutions meet business and operational needs.

### Responsibilities
- Represent the needs of a functional area or domain
- Review solution fit, usability, and readiness
- Provide subject matter expertise and decisions
- Validate requirements against operational realities
- Support handoff and adoption activities

### Goals
- Ensure solutions fit real-world operational needs
- Improve quality and adoption of outcomes
- Support clear ownership across business and technical domains

### Typical Communication
- Domain-specific design reviews
- Business readiness and acceptance checks
- Cross-functional issue discussions

### Interacts with existing roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers on validation of requirements and outcomes.
- Provides guidance to Developers and Project Managers on domain or technical constraints.
- Helps Project Sponsors and Stakeholder Representatives confirm business readiness.

---

## Stakeholder Representative

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Representatives ensure the interests of business, customer, or partner groups are represented throughout the project lifecycle. They help ensure decisions reflect stakeholder needs and concerns.

### Responsibilities
- Gather stakeholder feedback and concerns
- Represent broader audience needs during planning and review
- Help resolve trade-offs and acceptance concerns
- Support communication of status, impacts, and decisions
- Ensure decisions reflect stakeholder priorities

### Goals
- Maintain trust and transparency with impacted groups
- Improve alignment between project decisions and stakeholder expectations
- Reduce surprises during delivery and rollout

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder reviews and feedback sessions
- Change impact communications
- Decision and milestone updates

### Interacts with existing roles
- Shares feedback with Product Managers and Project Managers.
- Helps Project Sponsors understand stakeholder impact and risk.
- Works with Functional Leads and Developers to validate whether delivery meets stakeholder expectations.

- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These additional roles help define accountability, escalation paths, and ownership across project planning, delivery, and stakeholder engagement.
